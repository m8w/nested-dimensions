nested dimensionscontrol

Every Nested Dimensions window on this computer shows up here, and changes take effect in it straight away, even when it’s fullscreen in its own Space. Show brings a window to the front. Fill screen makes it cover its display while staying on the normal desktop. For true fullscreen, press ⌃⌘F inside that window; you can keep editing it from here.

The browser blocked opening a window. Press the button to open it.
